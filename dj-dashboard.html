📋 How It Works

1. Submit your cover information and photos.

2. Complete the cover-design payment.

3. Your request goes to PASONG Admin.

4. Admin designs the finished cover.

5. Admin attaches the finished artwork to your DJ mix.

6. Admin approves the DJ mix.

7. Your DJ mix goes online.
