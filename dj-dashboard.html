🎧 DJ Mix Sales

DJ mixes can be streamed freely on PASONG. There is no one-minute streaming limit for DJ mixes.

  • 🇺🇬 Uganda download: UGX 1,000
  • 🌍 Outside Uganda download: US$1
  • 💰 DJ receives 60%
  • 🏠 PASONG receives 40%

❤️ Tip DJ

Listeners can support DJs directly through the Tip DJ feature.

  • ❤️ DJ receives 70%
  • 🏠 PASONG receives 30%
DJ mix earnings, tips and withdrawals will be connected to the PASONG payment system.
