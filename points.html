🔐 Referral Protection

  • Rewards are not given simply for signing up.
  • A referral qualifies after the referred person completes their first qualifying song purchase.
  • PASONG can prevent self-referrals and duplicate rewards.
  • The same qualifying purchase should not generate multiple referral rewards.
  • Suspicious or fraudulent referral activity can be reviewed before points are credited.
