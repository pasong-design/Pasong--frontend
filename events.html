CLEAR & TRANSPARENT

How PASONG ticket commission works

The proposed platform fee is 5% of successfully paid ticket sales, deducted from the organiser's ticket revenue. The buyer does not pay an additional PASONG commission on top of the displayed ticket price.

The organiser receives the ticket revenue after the platform commission, subject to applicable fees and settlement terms.

Example only. Actual settlement must be handled by the backend after Pesapal payment verification, with refunds, payment-provider fees and organiser payouts handled under the applicable terms.
