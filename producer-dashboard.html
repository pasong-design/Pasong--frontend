📜 PASONG Producer Beat Agreement

1. Ownership

The Producer confirms that they own 100% of the beat and have the rights needed to license it through PASONG. PASONG does not take ownership of the beat.

2. Money Split

For every beat sale, the Producer receives 75% and PASONG receives 25%.

  • Example: UGX 10,000 non-exclusive licence → Producer UGX 7,500 / PASONG UGX 2,500.
  • Example: UGX 500,000 exclusive licence → Producer UGX 375,000 / PASONG UGX 125,000.

3. Non-Exclusive Licence

A non-exclusive beat may be licensed to multiple buyers. There is no fixed maximum number of non-exclusive sales. Each buyer receives the licence rights attached to the package they purchase.

4. Exclusive Licence

When an exclusive licence is sold, PASONG will remove the beat from new sales. Existing valid non-exclusive licences granted before the exclusive sale remain valid according to their licence terms.

An exclusive licence does not transfer copyright ownership unless a separate written agreement expressly transfers copyright ownership.

5. Payment

Producer earnings are paid to the registered MTN MoMo or Airtel Money details connected to the PASONG producer account. The minimum withdrawal amount is UGX 10,000. Payout timing is subject to PASONG processing, verification and payment-provider requirements.

6. Producer Responsibility

The Producer confirms that the beat is original or that the Producer has obtained all necessary rights, permissions and licences for every element used in the beat. The Producer must not upload stolen or unauthorized copyrighted material.

7. Buyer Protection

A buyer may use the beat only according to the licence package purchased. Buyers may not resell or redistribute the standalone beat. Required producer credit must be given where applicable.

8. Free Streaming

PASONG may provide a free streaming version for listening. PASONG may add its streaming protection tag and advertising to the free-streaming experience. The clean licensed file supplied after payment remains separate from the protected free-streaming version.

9. Content ID and Rights Management

PASONG may assist with rights-management or Content ID reporting where requested by the Producer and where the beat is eligible for such services.

10. Beat Removal

The Producer may request removal of a beat from PASONG. Removing a beat does not cancel licences that were already legally granted to buyers before removal.

11. Digital Acceptance

By checking the agreement box and uploading the beat, the Producer confirms that they have read and accepted this Producer Beat Agreement and agree to the terms stated above.

Agreement Version: PASONG-PRODUCER-BEAT-v2.0
